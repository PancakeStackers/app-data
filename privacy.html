Privacy Policy

[We The People] · Effective November 10, 2026
Developer: [Zachary Schadler] · Contact: [Schadlerpainting@gmail.com]

The short version. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. The developer does not collect or receive your address, your answers, or the representatives you choose. Those stay on your phone. The only information that leaves your phone is described in sections 3 and 4.

1. What the app stores on your phone

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your state and the House member you confirmed as your representative;
  • your Yea and Nay answers on bills;
  • a list of the bills the app has already shown you, and your sound on/off setting;
  • a saved copy of the public vote data, so the app works offline.

None of this is sent to the developer. You can erase your answers and saved representatives at any time with “Erase my answers and start over” on the Matches screen. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

2. Your answers may reveal political views

Your Yea and Nay answers can show your political opinions. That is why the app keeps them on your device and does not send them to the developer or to any other party.

3. Your address

When you type your address to find your representatives, the app sends it over an encrypted connection to the U.S. Census Bureau’s public geocoding service, which returns your congressional district. The app does not save your address. The Census Bureau will see the address you typed and your internet (IP) address, and handles them under its own policies.

4. Public vote data and links

The app downloads public records (bills, summaries, and how members of Congress voted) from a website hosted on GitHub Pages. That file contains nothing about you, but when your phone requests it, GitHub can see your IP address and basic device information, as with any website. Bill text links open government websites, such as Congress.gov, in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information, because we do not have it.
  • The app does not show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use your GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • The app does not require an account.

6. About the data

Bill and vote information comes from public government sources, including Congress.gov and the records of the U.S. House and Senate, and is provided as is. Summaries may be shortened or simplified, so please read the full text of a bill before relying on it. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government agency, political party, or candidate.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding accounts, notifications that require a server, or other services), we will update this policy and its effective date before releasing the change.
